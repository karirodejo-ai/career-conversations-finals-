Career Clarity Conversation Prep Sheet

Use these prompts before a coaching session, networking conversation, or important career decision.

1. What is happening now?

What feels unclear, frustrating, or ready to change?

2. What do I want next?

Describe the role, environment, impact, or direction you want to explore.

3. What evidence do I already have?

List three strengths, results, or experiences you can bring forward.

4. My next confident action

What is one action you can take in the next seven days?
